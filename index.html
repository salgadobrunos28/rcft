RELATIONAL CARTOGRAPHY

A generative system tracing the shifting relations between words, people, and territories. Each circle on the screen emerges from a collective attempt to articulate what a border signifies.

Rather than reproducing geography, the work approaches cartography as a living gesture - a drawing of relations, tensions, proximities and distances.

Through the accumulation of shared language, the piece becomes a mutable cartographic field: a space where meanings drift, connect, separate, and recombine, resisting fixed definitions.
