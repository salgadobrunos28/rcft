RELATIONAL CARTOGRAPHY

A generative system that traces the shifting relations between words, people and territories. The work invites each visitor to define in a single word what a border is.

Every answer becomes a node in a drifting field, where meanings move closer and apart without settling into a definition.

The piece adopts the visual grammar of data and surveillance systems but refuses their function, it accumulates language without turning it into actionable knowledge.
